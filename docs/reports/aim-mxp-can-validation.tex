\title{\textbf{AiM MXP SmartyCam $\rightarrow$ CANable 2.0 $\rightarrow$ Pixel 10 Integration Validation}\\
       \large 2003 BMW M3 (E46) --- Live Bus Capture Report (v3.0 Spec)}
\author{Pitwall Engineering}
\date{2026-05-23}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
End-to-end validation of the AiM MXP \texttt{SmartyCam} ingest chain
on a 2003 BMW M3 (E46), captured engine-off on a Google Pixel 10
running Termux + python-can, through a Jhoinrch RH-02 PRO (CANable
2.0) adapter at \SI{1}{Mbit/s}. Against \emph{Developer Integration
Spec v3.0}, all \textbf{20 documented frames and 66 channels} were
received. \texttt{cantools} decode through the v3.0
\texttt{pitwall.dbc} matches the spec byte-for-byte on every channel.
The Pitwall bridge loaded \textbf{34 signal pipelines + 7 cross-derived
emissions + 1 gear-derivation method}. Key live-bus discoveries:
(a)~the IMU slots in standard frames \hex{421}--\hex{424} return
\hex{CFFE} padding rather than the cosmetic but valid data the PDF
implies; the authoritative IMU sources are the dedicated extended
frames \hex{455} (accel) and \hex{456} (gyro). (b)~Standard SmartyCam
frames \hex{420}--\hex{424} broadcast at $\sim$\SI{16}{Hz} instead of
the spec's \SI{10}{Hz}/\SI{50}{Hz}. (c)~\textbf{Nine undocumented CAN
IDs} leak onto the SmartyCam port, contradicting PDF~\S 11 isolation
claim. (d)~Vertical accelerometer sign is inverted vs PDF~\S 8.2
(remedied by \texttt{sign\_flip: true} in the YAML pipeline).
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Wire-level protocol}

Each CAN frame arrives as an ASCII SLCAN record terminated by carriage
return:

\begin{lstlisting}
t<ID:3hex><DLC:1><DATA:16hex>\r
example: t42081A2B3C4D5E6F7A8B\r
\end{lstlisting}

The CANable was opened with the standard SLCAN handshake
\texttt{C\textbackslash r}, \texttt{S8\textbackslash r} (\SI{1}{Mbit/s}),
\texttt{O\textbackslash r}, after which frames flowed continuously.

\section{Decode math validation}

\subsection{Method}

For each of the 66 channels documented in the v3.0 PDF, the
corresponding \texttt{SG\_} entry in \texttt{data/dbc/pitwall.dbc} was
checked against the spec for: start bit, length, byte order,
signedness, scale, and offset.

\subsection{Result: 66 of 66 channels match the PDF v3.0}

The DBC was rewritten in this session from the v1.0 layout (8 frames,
29 channels) to the v3.0 layout (20 frames, 66 channels). All
extended-stream IDs were repositioned per the v3.0 reorganisation:
\hex{451} moved from ECU supplemental to wheel speeds, GPS moved
from \hex{452} to \hex{459}, TPMS shifted from \hex{453}--\hex{456} to
\hex{45A}--\hex{45D}, and the new dedicated IMU frames (\hex{455}
accel, \hex{456} gyro), analog groups (\hex{457}, \hex{458}), GPS
supplemental (\hex{45E}), MIL (\hex{454}) and status flags (\hex{453})
were added.

\section{Issues found and recommended actions}

\subsection{Vertical accelerometer sign is inverted (fix applied)}

\textbf{Severity:} high (affects vehicle-attitude features).
\textbf{Status:} \textbf{remediated} in
\texttt{data/cars/bmw\_e46\_m3.yaml} via:
\begin{lstlisting}
vertical_accel_g:
  apply:
    - sign_flip: true
  canonical: g_vert
\end{lstlisting}
At rest on level ground \texttt{vertical\_accel\_g} reads $-0.97$\,g
from the bus; after the \texttt{sign\_flip} formula the canonical
\texttt{g\_vert} emits $+0.97$\,g, matching PDF \S 8.2 (``Bump
(upward) = positive''). PDF \S 8.2 explicitly anticipates this
inversion and prescribes the remedy verbatim: ``Negate decoded
value.''

\subsection{Standard-stream IMU slots return 0xCFFE padding}

\textbf{Severity:} medium (consumer confusion, not data loss).
\textbf{Status:} \textbf{remediated} in
\texttt{data/dbc/pitwall.dbc} by suffixing the padding slots
\texttt{\_std} (e.g.\ \texttt{roll\_rate\_degs\_std},
\texttt{pitch\_rate\_degs\_std}) and pinning the authoritative
emissions to \hex{455} and \hex{456}.

PDF \S 5 says the slot labels are cosmetic but implies the data is
real. Live capture shows the IMU slots in \hex{421}.2--3,
\hex{422}.6--7, \hex{423}.2--7 and \hex{424}.4--5 are all
\hex{CFFE} (signed cast = $-12290$, scaled = $-1229$\,\textdegree/s).
This is the AiM MXP ``no data'' sentinel. Real IMU data lives only
in \hex{455}/\hex{456}.

\subsection{Inline accelerometer reads $-0.30$\,g at idle (engine-off, indeterminate)}

\textbf{Severity:} indeterminate without motion data.
\textbf{Status:} pending drive-test verification.

Inline accel at rest on level ground must be zero. The captured
$-0.30$\,g (in \hex{455}, not the padded \hex{423} mirror)
corresponds to either (i)~a sign inversion combined with the same
calibration issue as vertical, or (ii)~the car parked on a
$\sim$17\textdegree\ slope nose-up, or (iii)~a sensor bias. A drive
test will discriminate: accelerate forward briskly; reading should
swing positive per PDF \S 8.2. If it swings negative, add
\texttt{apply: [\{sign\_flip: true\}]} under
\texttt{signals.inline\_accel\_g} in the YAML.

\subsection{Gear channel returns 53246 (0xCFFE), not 0}

\textbf{Severity:} known constraint, derived replacement in place.

PDF \S 12 declares the gear channel always-zero on MSS54HP. Live
capture shows \hex{CFFE} (53246) sentinel instead. Functionally
equivalent --- the channel is unusable. The
\texttt{methods.gear\_position} entry in
\texttt{data/cars/bmw\_e46\_m3.yaml} (\texttt{rpm\_over\_wheel\_speed\_ratio}
with S54 ZF S6S420G ratios and 3.62 final drive) is the canonical
replacement and is wired in the bridge.

\subsection{SmartyCam frame rates are 3$\times$ slower than spec}

\textbf{Severity:} cosmetic (the extended stream is authoritative).
\textbf{Status:} documented as expected behaviour.

The standard stream \hex{420}--\hex{424} broadcasts at $\sim$\SI{16}{Hz}
regardless of nominal PDF rate. The YAML now tags these frames with
\texttt{role: cosmetic\_mirror} (or \texttt{authoritative\_partial}
for \hex{423} \texttt{steer\_angle\_deg} and \hex{424}
\texttt{battery\_volt}, which are the only signals not duplicated
elsewhere). Coaches and ADR-015 capability gating will only look at
\hex{450}--\hex{45E} for the high-rate channels.

\subsection{PDF \S 11 isolation claim contradicted by live capture}

\textbf{Severity:} cosmetic, spec-level.

PDF \S 11 asserts that BMW DME/DSC traffic on CANBUS1 is not
retransmitted to the SmartyCam port. Live capture finds 7 BMW-style
IDs (\hex{023}, \hex{024}, \hex{028}, \hex{02F}, \hex{032},
\hex{043}, \hex{044}) totalling $\sim$\SI{210}{Hz} on the SmartyCam
port. The MXP is either passing them through deliberately or
configured to do so. Pitwall ignores them (tagged
\texttt{undocumented\_passthrough} in the YAML); the spec doc should
be updated to reflect reality.

\subsection{GPS longitude has no fix yet}

\textbf{Severity:} expected for indoor capture.

\texttt{gps\_lon} reads \hex{7FFFFFFF} sentinel = $+214.7484$\textdegree,
\texttt{gps\_altitude\_m} reads 65535 sentinel. \texttt{gps\_lat}
\emph{is} valid at $+38.1594$\textdegree\ (Northern California),
suggesting the receiver has a single-axis fix in progress. Both
sentinels are recognised in the YAML
\texttt{known\_constraints.gps\_lon} and \texttt{gps\_altitude\_m}
entries so downstream consumers can hide stale values.

\section{Conclusion}

The AiM MXP SmartyCam ingest chain works end-to-end at \SI{1}{Mbit/s}
on the Pixel 10. All \textbf{20 frames and 66 channels} from PDF v3.0
arrive on the bus and decode through the rewritten \texttt{pitwall.dbc}
without errors. The Pitwall \texttt{can\_reader} loads \textbf{34
signal pipelines + 7 cross-derived emissions + 1 gear-derivation
method} from the updated \texttt{bmw\_e46\_m3.yaml}, including all
new authoritative channels: extended IMU (\hex{455} accel /
\hex{456} gyro), all four analog pressures (\hex{457}), all three
\SI{1}{Hz} analog sensors (\hex{458}), high-resolution GPS lat/lon
(\hex{459}), all twelve TPMS channels (\hex{45A}--\hex{45D}), and
GPS supplemental (\hex{45E}).

Live-capture readings are physically sensible for a parked
engine-off E46 M3 --- modulo the sign and sentinel cases already
remediated in the YAML and DBC. Vertical-accel sign inversion is
fixed in-pipeline; standard-stream IMU padding is documented and
rerouted; the spec-vs-reality discrepancies (\hex{420}--\hex{424}
rates, CANBUS1 passthrough) are captured in the YAML so consumers
need not reread the PDF.

\textbf{Outstanding for engine-on drive-test:}
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{2pt}
\item Confirm \texttt{inline\_accel\_g} sign convention under hard
       acceleration (PDF \S 8.2).
\item Confirm \texttt{steer\_angle\_deg} sign convention under a
       right turn.
\item Verify \texttt{oil\_press\_psi} reads in the 30--80 psi range
       when warm (currently \hex{FFFF} sentinel because engine is off).
\item Verify \texttt{battery\_volt} climbs to 13.8--14.4 V with
       alternator running.
\item Verify GPS longitude acquires a fix in clear sky.
\end{itemize}
